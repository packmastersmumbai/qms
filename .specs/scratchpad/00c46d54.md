### Trivial actions folded in
- No standalone "create file" / "add column" steps. Reseed data folded into the seed edit step. inferLocType edit is small but verification-worthy (typing correctness has a real failure mode -> RISK-1) and is a shared prerequisite consumed by the map step, so it earns its own small step OR merges into reseed. Decision: MERGE L0b into the reseed step (Step 1) — both are "make slots exist AND type correctly", one coherent verifiable unit, avoids a thin agent pair. This keeps Step 1 as Small-Medium with two real success criteria.

## Stage 3/4: Strategy
Mixed. Bottom-up for data/backend (seed, inferLocType, saveMaterial, fit engine); the map is
inside-out (data wiring first, then interactions). Phase 1 chain (Steps 1-3) is independently
shippable and delivers all 4 goals. Phase 2 chain (Steps 4-6) is gated and starts with the
saveMaterial regression fix which BLOCKS the geometry step.

## Stage 5: Granularity decisions
- 6 steps. Each does verification-worthy work (heatmap logic, movement wiring, truncation regression, fit math with asserts).
- Step 2 (the map) is the one Large step — multiple interactions (heatmap, PIP, search, KPI, tile-pick, QR fallback, themes). Considered splitting into "static map" + "interactions" but the map has no value without at least tap+heatmap, and a static-only map gives the verifier little beyond "148 rects render". Keeping as ONE Large step with rich subtasks + success criteria per goal; it is the natural vertical slice. If it exceeds Large in practice, split at the QR/tile-pick boundary (noted as risk).
- No Too-Small steps. inferLocType merged into Step 1.
